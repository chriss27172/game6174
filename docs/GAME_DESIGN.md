# NEON//REIGN — Game Design v0.1

## Vision
A fast, strategic, cyberpunk collectible card battler. Fun first; blockchain is optional for gameplay.

## Core MVP
- Five free starter cards per player, reusable forever.
- Three city sectors. Each turn players deploy one card into a sector and resolve abilities.
- Five rounds; control at least two sectors to win.
- Each card has energy cost, power, faction and one ability.
- Players gain energy each round (1, 2, 3, 4, 5).
- No pay-to-play requirement; a starter deck must remain viable.
- Prototype: local player vs deterministic AI. Later: online PvP, rankings and trading.

## Collectibles and monetization
- Free starter cards are playable without a wallet.
- Future paid cards can have different strengths, but must have meaningful counters and a fair matchmaking environment.
- Do not sell randomized paid packs in the MVP.
- No token launch, real-money wagers, or cash prizes in the MVP.
- Cosmetics and fixed-price card sales are safer early experiments than loot boxes.
- Keep card combat calculations server-authoritative for eventual PvP.

## Art direction
- Follow the first approved female hacker card: dark cinematic cyberpunk, black and deep red, neon magenta, selective electric blue.
- Grounded silhouettes, striking character identities, premium collectible framing.
- Avoid generic robots and overly glossy sci-fi aesthetics.

## Success metrics
- A complete match is playable.
- A first-time player understands the rules within 2 minutes.
- Test with 10 players and measure match completion, rematches and perceived fairness.
